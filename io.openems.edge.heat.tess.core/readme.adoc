= Thermal ESS Core

Shared calculation helpers for thermal energy storage systems.

The `TemperatureProfile` estimates the vertical temperature profile of a storage tank from temperatures
known at certain heights, e.g. a sensor in an immersion sleeve plus the supply and return temperatures of
a heat pump circulating through the tank. Between the known points the profile is interpolated linearly;
above the highest and below the lowest point it continues constantly. Its mean is the basis of the
estimated energy content.

The `NetThermalPowerEstimator` derives the net thermal power from the change of the mean temperature over
a sliding window, which smooths the quantization of temperature sensors. Steps of the mean caused by
additional known temperatures can be rebased, so corrections of the estimate do not appear as power.

The `StratifiedTank` models a stratified water storage tank as a stack of nodes: water entering and
leaving through connections moves as plug flow, heat exchangers and heating elements heat individual
nodes, and temperature inversions are mixed out. It serves as the physical plant of simulations and can
predict the tank state from known heat flows.
