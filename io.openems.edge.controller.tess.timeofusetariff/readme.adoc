= Time-of-Use Tariff Controller for Thermal Energy Storage

Charges a `ManagedThermalEss` (single storage or `Tess.Cluster`) in cheap electricity price
quarters beyond its target temperature, up to the hardware maximum temperature. The stored
heat extends the time until the storage cools down to its minimum temperature, so the
Heating device does not need to run during expensive hours.

The controller integrates with the OpenEMS Energy Scheduler as an `EnergySchedulable`:
the genetic optimizer schedules `CHARGE_GRID` periods on price valleys. The simulation
models the uncontrollable heat demand per quarter — predicted by the Predictor framework
(e.g. Similarday model) on the storage's `ThermalDischargePower` channel — and the
autonomous two-point reheating of the Heating device below the minimum temperature, which
is what makes pre-charging profitable. The grid energy of a thermal charge is derived via
the configured thermal efficiency (thermal output per electrical input, e.g. a heat pump
COP).

Outside of `CHARGE_GRID` periods the controller writes nothing: the Heating devices remain
autonomous within their temperature band, and a higher-priority controller (e.g. a future
PV-surplus controller) may request charging via `TargetThermalPowerGreaterOrEquals`.
