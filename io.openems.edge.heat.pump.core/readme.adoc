= Heat Pump Core

Shared calculation helpers for Heat Pumps.

The `HeatPumpPerformanceEstimator` estimates a Heat Pump's live Coefficient of Performance (COP)
from its nominal rating point, the current sink (flow/buffer) temperature and the part-load ratio.
It is intended for both real drivers whose devices do not report a COP as telemetry, and for
Heat Pump simulators. The estimate combines a Carnot-based temperature term (using an assumed
quality grade) with a documented part-load curve; all parameters other than the assumed quality
grade are derived from the nominal rating point.
